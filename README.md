# One Star, Written in Sap

A houseplant leaves a one-star review of its owner: watered every Sunday whether thirsty or not, apologised to the fern instead, and squeezed into a pot two sizes too small that he calls "cosy".

## Entry

`main.py` writes one line — a genuine one-star review, under 280 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. The plant's grievance is the centre, told in a plant's own view of water, pots and windowsills.

Standard library only, no network, no third-party packages.
